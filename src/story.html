The Story

He locked the gym.They unlocked their futures.

Richmond, California. January 1999. An undefeated team, a signed contract and a coach who believed the grades mattered more than the games.

Richmond Oilers · Season record at the lockout

Undefeatedwasn't enough.

Fifteen of his forty-five players had fallen short of the contract. Coach Carter cancelled practice, forfeited two games and turned the team's gym time into study hall. The doors reopened after eight study sessions.

The record, year by year
Timeline

From Richmond
to the world.

1959

Born. Raised in Richmond.

One of nine children — seven sisters and a brother. He would go on to play for Richmond High himself, then build a business before coming home to coach.

1997

Takes over Richmond High

Head coach of the Oilers. Every player signs a contract: a 2.3 GPA (above the 2.0 the district required), the front row of every class, and at least ten hours of study a week.

1999

The lockout

Early January, the team is 13–0. Fifteen players are short of the contract. He padlocks the gym, cancels practice and forfeits two games. His own son, Damien, is among the players benched. NPR, CNN, the New York Times and the Los Angeles Times cover it.

2001

Fifteen for fifteen

The Los Angeles Times reports that all fifteen players who missed the requirement have gone on to higher education.

2002

Olympic torchbearer

Carries the Olympic flame for Richmond and the Bay Area on the road to the Salt Lake City Winter Games.

2005

Coach Carter opens at #1

Paramount Pictures and MTV Films release Coach Carter on January 14. It opens at number one with $24.2 million and reaches $76.7 million worldwide. Coach Ken Carter and Damien Carter serve as executive consultants. It opens the Palm Springs International Film Festival.

2012

Yes Ma'am, No Sir

Business Plus (Hachette Book Group) publishes his book: The 12 Essential Steps for Success in Life, with an audio edition read by Coach.

Today

The standard goes national

Universities, business summits, school districts, churches and youth organizations from coast to coast — and a new chapter on screen: Coach Carter II.
