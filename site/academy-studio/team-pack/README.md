# Coach Carter Academy Team Pack

Offline production source for a small-group reading and commitment session. The pack is designed to sit beside 25 copies of the published book and is intentionally original: it does not reproduce cover art, book text, chapter references, or quotations.

## Files

- `commitment-card-front.svg` — 5 × 7 in front face.
- `commitment-card-back.svg` — 5 × 7 in writable back face.
- `facilitator-guide.html` — finished, print-ready discussion guide.
- `pack-label.svg` — 6 × 4 in label for the accessory mailer.
- `production-spec.md` — packing, photo, and print-proof gates.

Print the two card SVGs at 100% on 130–160 lb uncoated cover stock. Keep the writing areas blank; the card is completed in person.
