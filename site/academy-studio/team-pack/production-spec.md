# Team Pack production specification

## Pack contents

- 25 copies of the published book, supplied by the organizer.
- 25 two-sided 5 × 7 in Commitment Cards: one front and one back per participant.
- 1 Facilitator Guide for the session leader.
- 25 black or maroon archival pens.
- 1 bone or kraft corrugated accessory mailer with the 6 × 4 in Team Pack label. Start the fit proof at 9 × 12 × 2 in inside dimensions. Pack the cards in five sets of five, pens secured beside the cards and the guide on top.
- Ship or source the books separately. For a single bulk order, use the distributor’s book cartons and send the accessory mailer alongside them. Confirm carton dimensions and actual shipping weight from a packed sample.

## Print specification

- Commitment Card: 5 × 7 in, 130–160 lb uncoated cover, maroon front and warm-bone back.
- Print at 100% with no “fit to page”; trim to the artboard. Keep a 0.125 in safe area.
- Facilitator Guide: US Letter, uncoated 28–32 lb text stock, duplex or booklet output.
- Carton label: 6 × 4 in matte permanent paper. Request 0.125 in bleed by extending the solid background beyond the trim; retain the border position. Apply to a clean, flat carton lid.

## Gates

1. **Source gate:** confirm the two SVG faces open correctly and no monogram or reproduced book material appears.
2. **Print-proof gate:** print one front/back pair; verify trim, writing lines, legibility, maroon/gold contrast, and front/back orientation.
3. **Writing gate:** test the back with the selected pen; confirm ink does not feather or show through to the front.
4. **Pack gate:** count 25 cards, 25 pens and one guide in the accessory mailer. Confirm 25 books separately. Check lid closure, shake test the mailer and record the packed weight before photography.
5. **Session gate:** facilitator has a timer, check-in date, and a safe method for voluntary sharing.
6. **Photo gate:** shoot one overhead flat-lay, one hand-writing detail, and one sealed pack image. Avoid showing participant names without consent.

## Suggested product language

“A guided reading-and-action pack for a small group: published book supplied separately, 25 writable Commitment Cards, facilitator guide, and session materials.”
