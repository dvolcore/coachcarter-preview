Review note: series portrait — replace with Coach's official press headshot before launch

Coach Ken Carter is an educator, author and speaker whose 1999 decision to lock his undefeated Richmond High basketball team out of the gym until its grades improved made national news and inspired Paramount Pictures' 2005 film Coach Carter, which opened at number one at the U.S. box office. Two years later, the Los Angeles Times reported that all fifteen players who had fallen short of the contract were in higher education.

Carter is the author of Yes Ma'am, No Sir: The 12 Essential Steps for Success in Life (Business Plus, 2012), founder of the Coach Ken Carter Foundation, a 2002 Olympic torchbearer, and a recipient of the NAACP Impact Citizen of the Year and Harvard Club Distinguished Secondary Educator awards. He speaks to corporations, universities, school districts and youth organizations across the United States, and is executive producer of the series Coach Carter Academy.

Usage

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the “C” around every mark.
  • Gold on carbon or maroon; carbon on chalk. Never place the wordmark on busy photography without a scrim.
  • Don't redraw, outline, stretch or recolor the marks.
  • Coach Carter Academy gear carries the wordmark and the KC monogram. There is no CC or CCA varsity mark; “Academy” is a secondary descriptor, set deliberately and never fused into the master logo.
  • Film stills, poster art and actor likenesses from the 2005 film are not part of this kit.
