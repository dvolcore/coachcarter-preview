Coach Ken Carter

Coach founded the Coach Ken Carter Foundation to provide education, training and mentoring for young people. The plan is to reconstitute it as the nonprofit home for the Contract programs — built on the same four rules: accountability, discipline, respect and education first.

01

Mentoring

Consistent adults who hold young people to a standard — and show up when it's hard.

02

Study first

Study hall, tutoring and academic contracts that put the classroom ahead of the court.

03

Life skills

The twelve steps of Yes Ma'am, No Sir, taught to students and the adults around them.

04

Pathways

College, trade and career paths — because every player in college is the target, not the exception.
