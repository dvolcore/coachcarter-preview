01 — The series
Logline

The man whotaught them to finish.

Coach Ken Carter returns to college basketball — as a player — to finish what he started. When his comeback threatens a young teammate's place, and his dream coaching job arrives with a 24-hour deadline, he has to live by the standards that made him famous.

He taught a generation accountability. Now the jersey is his.

The true story
January 21, 2015

It really
happened.

Around age 55, Coach Ken Carter went back to college basketball — as a player. Baylor's official record for January 21, 2015 shows an 81–61 Baylor win over Huston-Tillotson, the game at the center of that comeback.

That is the true story behind Coach Carter Academy. The series is drama built on it.
