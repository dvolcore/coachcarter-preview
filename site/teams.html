01 — The program
What a team gets

Built to be
run on Monday.

A defined scope your staff can deliver: Coach's material, your facilitator, your players. Court demonstrations and family stories support the lessons.

01

Opening session

The team, the support adults and the facilitator agree the standard together.

02

Written commitments

A reciprocal agreement: player, parent, coach and team each sign their part.

03

Six weekly activities

One focused activity a week, from the baseline to the next-season plan.

04

Facilitator guide

So a local coach or facilitator can run the weekly process with confidence.

05

Weekly check-ins

A short check-in each week to review progress and catch obstacles early.

06

Closing review

The team explains what changed — and what still needs work.

Who it's for
  • Athletic directors
  • Club directors
  • Community operators
Straight talk
  • Remote delivery, up to 20 participants. A personal appearance by Coach is not included.
  • Academic goals are set with your school and families — the 1999 GPA number is not a universal standard for today.
  • Your facilitator keeps the completion record. We don't collect individual student grades.
  • We report participation and how the process was used. Six weeks can't prove better grades or graduation rates, so we won't claim it.
02 — The commitment
A modern Academy commitment

Everybody
signs something.

A proposed new teaching tool — not Coach's original contract. The six-week pilot tests whether a simple reciprocal agreement helps a team follow through. The original contract lives in the Archive.

The ContractSix-week team program · working draft
Player
  • Set a personal learning goal and a team goal.
  • Attend agreed activities.
  • Communicate an obstacle early.
  • Complete the weekly check-in.
  • Ask for help when needed.

  • Revisit progress weekly.

03 — Six weeks
The sequence

Week by week.

One clear job per week. The stories and court work support each lesson; they don't have to become a forced metaphor for every activity.

  1. Week 1Agree the standard

    Set the standard together and record the baseline.

  2. Week 2Show up

    Show up — and ask for help.

  3. Week 3Communicate

    Practice communication as a team.

  4. Week 4Decide

    Make a difficult team decision.

  5. Week 5Serve

    Complete the team's service action.

  6. Week 6Review

    Review commitments and plan the next season.

The Seven Sisters

He named his plays
after his sisters.
