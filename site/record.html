Back home

Richmond High School · December 2024
Back on the Richmond bench.

In December 2024, more than twenty-five years after the lockout, Coach returned to the Richmond High sideline. Local reporting in January 2026 described a handoff of the bench, with Coach preferring to help from afar. Photos of his first game back are in the Getty archive.
