The original contract
Richmond High · 1998–99 season

The document
behind the lockout.

Every player signed it. Their parents signed it. Coach signed it alongside them. Here is what the record can prove about it — and what it can't yet.

What's on the record

  • 2.3 GPAThe academic threshold. Coach's book, Yes Ma'am, No Sir
  • Every classAttend all of them and sit at the front. Coach's book
  • Game dayWhite shirt and tie at every game. Coach's book
  • The workHomework, study hall and community work. Coach's 2005 interview
  • SignaturesPlayers, parents and Coach. Coach's 2005 interview
  • 10 hoursOf study a week, plus the front row of every class. San Francisco Chronicle and CNN, January 1999

What we're still looking for

Coach has described it as a nineteen-point pledge. The signed original has not yet been located in an authenticated scan — so this archive lists only the terms that Coach's own accounts and the 1999 reporting confirm, and does not fill in the rest.

Partial typed versions circulate online. Their source is unverified, and we don't reproduce them.

Any contract you see elsewhere on this site is a clearly labeled modern edition, written in the spirit of the original. It is not a copy of the 1999 document.
