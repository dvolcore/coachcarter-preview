# Coach Carter Academy · Collector production kit

Offline production artwork for a future signed-game-ball release and a clearly labeled modern Contract edition. These files are templates, not proof of inventory, authenticity, or a completed product.

## Signed game ball packout

- Use a full-size, retail-ready basketball in a rigid two-piece archival box with an acid-free foam cradle. Do not print a signature into the ball or certificate.
- Photograph the actual ball before listing: 45° hero, signature macro, valve/model panel, serial or batch mark, and every edge of the box. Include a scale reference in the internal record, never in the customer image.
- Complete every bracketed field on `signed-ball-certificate.svg` from the physical item and Coach-approved documentation. If a field is unknown, leave it blank rather than infer it.
- Numbering must be one-to-one: certificate number = inventory record = box label. Retain a private scan and chain-of-custody log.

## Contract edition packout

- The insert and sleeve are original presentation materials. They do not reproduce, summarize, or imitate the missing 1999 signed original.
- Mount any future modern edition flat behind a clear, acid-free, UV-filtering sleeve; never laminate or permanently glue it.
- Keep the original contract (if later authenticated) physically separate from the modern edition and never use the insert to imply authenticity.
- Photograph the assembled package, sleeve front/back, numbered colophon, and edition surface under even neutral light. No AI or generated product image should be used as product proof.

See `collector-photo-spec.md` for capture and evidence requirements.
