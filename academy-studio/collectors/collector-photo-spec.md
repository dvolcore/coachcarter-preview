# Collector product photography and evidence spec

## Signed game ball

Capture the actual item, not a rendering:

1. Hero: ball in box, 45° view, 4:5 crop, neutral bone background.
2. Signature: tight macro with no glare and the complete signed panel in frame.
3. Identity: manufacturer, model, size, valve and serial/batch marks.
4. Packout: open box, cradle, certificate, and numbered label together.
5. Condition: six-side rotation and any imperfections.

Record filename, capture date, camera/operator, item number, and SHA-256 hash. Retain RAW files and an untouched master export.

## Contract modern edition

Capture the physical sample:

1. Sleeve front and back, square-on.
2. Numbered colophon and any Coach-approved commentary, square-on.
3. Edition front/back/interior, with no reflections.
4. Mounted package in its protective sleeve.
5. Scale and material detail (paper, foil, binding, or embossing).

Product pages may show only approved exports. Do not claim “original,” “1999 document,” “authenticated,” or “signed” until documentary evidence and a Coach-approved record exist.
