01 — The Man
In his words

“Education was always my goal. I just used basketball as the hook.”

Coach Ken Carter grew up in Richmond, California, played for Richmond High, built a business, then came home to coach. When his 13–0 team stopped meeting the contract they signed, he padlocked the gym. The country called it crazy. Two years later, the Los Angeles Times reported, all fifteen who had fallen short were in higher education.

Chapter one
13–0

Undefeated. Then locked out.

Fifteen of his forty-five players fell short of the grades they promised. Coach Carter chained the gym doors, cancelled practice and called off a game. Study hall replaced the court. A week later, all forty-five made the grade — no forfeits.

Chapter two
2.3

A contract, not a suggestion.

A 2.3 GPA. A seat in the front row of every class. Ten hours of study a week. Every player signed, and so did their parents. Every player was held to it — including his own son.

Chapter three
NPR. CNN.

Loved on Friday. Hated by Monday.

NPR, CNN, the New York Times and the Los Angeles Times carried the story nationwide. Some called it a stunt. At Richmond’s first game back, Governor Gray Davis said, “I think he’s a hero.”

Chapter four
#1

The story America paid to see.

Paramount's Coach Carter opened at number one in the country with $24.2 million and went on to $76.7 million worldwide. Coach called it ninety-eight-and-a-half percent accurate.

Chapter five
Next.

Two decades of keynotes across the country, a book from Hachette, and a new series — Coach Carter Academy. The same contract, now written for companies, classrooms and teams.

03 — The Code
What the name stands for

Four rules.No exceptions.

“Coach Carter” has meant one thing for twenty-five years: a standard you sign and then live up to. Every keynote, product and partnership is built on these four.

I

Accountability

You signed it. You own it. No excuses, no exemptions — the star player and the coach's son included.

“I'm going to hold you accountable.”
II

Discipline

Front row. On time. Ten hours a week nobody sees. Greatness is built in the reps before the lights come on.

“Average is just not good enough. Period.”
III

Respect

Yes ma'am. No sir. Respect for others, your community and your environment comes before any goal of your own.

“Kindness and politeness and respect will never ever go out of style.”
IV

Education First

The game is the hook. The diploma, the degree and the career are the win. Basketball ends; what you learn does not.

“I believe if you see it, you can be it.”
