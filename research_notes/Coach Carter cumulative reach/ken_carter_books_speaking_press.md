## 1. Books: titles, publishers, editions, sales

### Takeaway
The briefing guessed "Yes, Yes, Y'all" and "Lead by Example". No such Carter books turned up. His one major-publisher book is *Yes Ma'am, No Sir: The 12 Essential Steps for Success in Life* (Hachette Book Group, Business Plus imprint, February 20, 2012). His other titles are self-published. No sales figures or bestseller-list appearances were found. Goodreads shows only about 74 ratings, which suggests modest sales.

### Inferences
- About 74 Goodreads ratings for a 2012 Big-Five title points to low-thousands to low-tens-of-thousands of lifetime copies. Readers rate on Goodreads at very roughly 1–5% of buyers for nonfiction (rule of thumb, unsourced). 74 ÷ 0.05 ≈ 1,500 and 74 ÷ 0.01 ≈ 7,400 copies, so call it ~1.5K–10K. **ESTIMATE, low confidence**; treat as an order of magnitude only. Event bulk sales at keynotes could push this higher.
- For the audience tally, books are a rounding error next to film, TV and speaking.

### Gaps
- No BookScan, publisher sales or bestseller data was found.
- No translations were found. WorldCat holdings were not checked, and Amazon review counts were not retrieved.
- "Yes, Yes, Y'all" and "Lead by Example" turned up nothing in search. They may be confused with other authors' titles. Ask Coach/Ralph where the titles came from.

## 2. Speaking career: volume, bureaus, fees, audiences

### Takeaway
At least eight bureaus list him. Advertised fees run about $10K–$30K live (estimates, not completed deals). The only frequency claim found is "more than 125 times per year", from a bureau bio, which is self-promotional and unverified. The "30+" and "82" counts from the briefing were not found in any source.

### Cited Findings
- **REPORTED:** "Speaks more than 125 times per year, mainly for youth groups and educational panels worldwide." The same page says he coached Richmond 1997–2002, has coached SlamBall's Rumble since 2002, and opened the Coach Carter Impact Academy in January 2012. — [SpeakInc profile](https://speakinc.com/speakers/coach-ken-carter)
- **REPORTED (fee ranges):**
  - All American Speakers: $10,000–$20,000 live, under $10,000 virtual; travels from Waco, TX. The page was 403 to fetch, so this comes from the search snippet. — [All American Speakers](https://allamericanspeakers.com/speakers/4377/Coach-Ken-Carter)
  - BigSpeak: $10,001–$20,000 (US). — [BigSpeak](https://bigspeak.com/?p=686)
  - Executive Speakers: $15,000–$20,000 in person. — [Executive Speakers](https://executivespeakers.com/speaker/ken-carter)
  - Gotham Artists: $20,000–$30,000. — [Gotham Artists](https://gothamartists.com/coach_ken_carter)
  - Premiere Speakers: "contact for pricing". — [Premiere Speakers](https://premierespeakers.com/ken_carter)
- **REPORTED:** Other bureau listings — [AAE Speakers](https://www.aaespeakers.com/speakers/coach-ken-carter), [Sports Speakers 360](https://www.sportsspeakers360.com/speaker/coach-ken-carter.php), [Celebrity Talent](https://www.celebritytalent.net/sampletalent/6144/ken-carter/). Keppler and WSB listings were not found.
- **REPORTED (named audiences):**
  - BigSpeak testimonials from PHCC National (Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors, Director of Education & Training) and Frank Colonna, Vice Mayor of Long Beach, CA. BigSpeak also says he is "rated as one of the top speakers by Hunger for Success". — [BigSpeak](https://bigspeak.com/?p=686)
  - A YouTube video shows him at the Mississippi Economic Council "MEC Powerplay 2015" annual meeting. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0328uq7KafA)
  - A Hudson Union Society interview appears in search results.
  - Playbook S05: Rome City School District appearance, Feb 27, 2024. S06: podcast, Dec 23, 2024. S51: KSNV reported school visits and a community screening in October 2026.
- **REPORTED:** He was already a paid motivational speaker running seminars before 1999 and got "lucrative lecture opportunities" after the lockout. — [JRank](https://biography.jrank.org/pages/2792/Carter-Ken.html)

### Inferences (ESTIMATE: cumulative live attendees, 1999–2026)
No source counts total attendees. Three scenarios:
- **Conservative:** 30 talks/yr × 25 yrs (2000–2025) = 750 talks × 300 avg attendees = **~225,000**
- **Mid:** 60 talks/yr × 25 = 1,500 talks × 400 = **~600,000**
- **Bio-claim ceiling:** 125/yr × 25 = 3,125 talks × 400 = **~1.25 million**

The ceiling takes the self-reported 125/yr at face value, and it is probably inflated. School assemblies (300–1,500) and corporate keynotes (200–2,000) bracket the 300–400 average, which is an assumption. **Recommended tally input: ~0.25M–0.6M live attendees (ESTIMATE)**, flagged as unverified.

### Gaps
- No verified count of engagements and no client list beyond the testimonials above.
- No NBA or Fortune 500 audience could be documented.
- Coach's own booking records would be the only real source.

## 5. The 2015 Baylor/Huston-Tillotson game, FIBA 2026, the Impact Academy, awards

### Takeaway
**Correction to the briefing: Carter did NOT play in the Jan 21, 2015 game.** The plan was for him to play for Huston-Tillotson at about 56, tied to a planned second film. HBCU Sports reports that plan fell through and no footage was shot. Baylor still played the game and won 81–61. The FIBA Foundation (Switzerland) exhibits Coach Carter film memorabilia; that is recognition of the film, with no visitor numbers. His personal honors are regional and civic, plus a 2002 Olympic torch leg.

### Cited Findings
- **VERIFIED:** Baylor beat Huston-Tillotson 81–61 on Jan 21, 2015 (Rico Gathers 28 rebounds, a Baylor record). — [Baylor Lariat](https://baylorlariat.com/2015/01/21/rico-gathers-powers-no-21-baylor-past-huston-tillotson)
- **REPORTED:** Details of the failed plan:
  - The plan was for Carter to play "this year at age 56". It "fell through" and no movie footage was filmed.
  - Baylor's SID David Kaye said plans existed for a second movie, and Baylor hosted the game as a favor to Carter.
  - The story was originally reported by Yahoo! Sports.

  — [HBCU Sports, Jan 22, 2015](https://hbcusports.com/2015/01/22/huston-tillotson-baylor-matchup-was-supposed-to-be-part-of-featured-film/)
- Note: the playbook (S08) cites Baylor postgame quotes about enrollment and pending NAIA eligibility. That is consistent with an attempted comeback that did not reach the court.
- **VERIFIED:** The FIBA Foundation's "From the Court to the Big Screen" exhibition is at the Patrick Baumann House of Basketball in Mies, Switzerland. It includes Coach Carter signed posters, jerseys and lobby cards. The "21 Years of Coach Carter" article is dated ©2026, and the playbook dates it Jan 14, 2026. No visitor numbers are given, and no Ken Carter involvement is stated. — [FIBA Foundation](https://foundation.fiba.basketball/en/news/21-years-of-coach-carter-a-legacy-of-discipline-education-and-inspiration); [exhibition launch](https://foundation.fiba.basketball/en/news/fiba-foundation-launches-its-new-exhibition-from-the-court-to-the-big-screen)
- **REPORTED (Coach Carter Impact Academy, Marlin, TX):** A boys' boarding school planned for up to 150 boys in grades 8–12. — [George Fox](https://www.georgefox.edu/featured_stories/Ken%20Carter.html). SpeakInc says it opened in January 2012; some mirrors say it was abandoned by 2012. **The sources conflict and the status is unresolved.**
- **REPORTED (awards):**
  - San Francisco Bay Area Entrepreneur of the Year (2001)
  - 2002 Salt Lake City Winter Olympics torchbearer
  - CityFlight magazine "Ten Most Influential African Americans in the Bay Area" (2000)
  - NAACP Impact Citizen of the Year
  - Leadership Award from SF Mayor Willie Brown

  — [Encyclopedia.com](https://encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/carter-ken); [SpeakInc](https://speakinc.com/speakers/coach-ken-carter)

### Inferences
- The 2015 episode is a story asset (a documentary hook), not an audience event. Coverage reach was niche (Yahoo! Sports, HBCU Sports, Baylor media).
