# Coach Carter (2005): Reach Through Theatrical, Home Video and Linear TV (Streaming and YouTube Excluded)

Date-stamped 2026-11-10. Runtime used: 136 min = 2.2667 h (rounded to 2.27 h per the brief).
Labels: **VERIFIED** = primary data page seen this session; **REPORTED** = secondary source or search snippet only; **ESTIMATE** = my calculation, formula shown.

## 1. Box office: domestic, international, worldwide, and admissions

### Takeaway
Domestic gross is $67,264,877 (VERIFIED, both trackers). That works out to about **10.49 million US/Canada tickets** at the 2005 NATO average price of $6.41. European admissions are **668,204** (VERIFIED, Lumiere). Total worldwide theatrical admissions come to an ESTIMATED **about 11.8 million (range 11.7M–12.2M)**, or **about 26.9 million viewing hours**.

### Cited Findings
- Domestic gross is **$67,264,877** (VERIFIED). It is identical on [Box Office Mojo](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0393162/) and [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Coach-Carter-(2005)).
- The two trackers give slightly different international and worldwide totals (all VERIFIED):
  - Box Office Mojo: international **$9,404,677**; worldwide **$76,669,892** in the summary, or $76,669,554 in the release-group table. [BOM](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0393162/)
  - The Numbers: international **$9,400,630**; worldwide **$76,665,507**. [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Coach-Carter-(2005))
  - **The playbook's ~$9,400,630 matches The Numbers exactly.** BOM is $4,047 higher. [MPA theatrical market statistics PDF](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/091af5d6-faf7-4f58-9a8e-405466c1c5e5.pdf); [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2006/03/14/bigscreen-slump/)
- The Numbers' inflation-adjusted domestic gross is **$125,505,142** (VERIFIED). [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Coach-Carter-(2005))
  - That figure ÷ 10,493,740 tickets = $11.96, which is the current-year average ticket price The Numbers applies.
  - So the adjustment is a ticket-count conversion, consistent with the ~10.49M figure below.

### Inferences (ESTIMATES, with math)
- **Domestic admissions** = $67,264,877 ÷ $6.41 = **10,493,740 tickets** (ESTIMATE, high confidence).
  - Nearly all grosses fell in Jan–Apr 2005, so the 2005 price applies.
  - The 4-day MLK weekend included some lower-priced matinee and child tickets. These are already reflected in the national average.
- **Domestic theatrical hours** = 10,493,740 × 2.27 = **23,820,791 hours**.
- **Europe:** use Lumiere's 668,204 actual admissions (no estimate needed).
  - Cross-check: BOM's European grosses for the same 12 Lumiere countries sum to $5,350,146.
  - $5,350,146 ÷ 668,151 = **$8.01 per admission**, a plausible 2005 European average.
- **Rest of world:** non-European international gross = $9,404,677 − $5,350,146 = **$4,054,531**, covering APAC, LatAm, South Africa and UAE.
  - Admissions at an assumed average ticket price:
    - $8 → 506,816
    - $6 (central) → 675,755
    - $4 → 1,013,633
  - The price is an assumption. Japan, Australia and Singapore were higher-price markets; Mexico, the Philippines and Central America were lower.
- **Worldwide theatrical admissions** = 10,493,740 + 668,204 + rest of world:
  - Low: **11,668,761**
  - Central: **11,837,700**
  - High: **12,175,577**
- **Worldwide theatrical hours** = admissions × 2.27:
  - Low: **26.49M**
  - Central: **26.87M**
  - High: **27.64M**
- Caveat: admissions are tickets, not unique people. Repeat viewings make unique theatrical viewers somewhat fewer than tickets. No repeat-rate data was found, so present it as "≈11.8M theatrical admissions", not "11.8M people".

### Gaps
- Lumiere has no admissions data outside Europe.
  - Korea's KOBIS would have actual admissions for South Korea; it was not checked.
  - Australia and Japan admissions were not found.
- The Numbers' "2005 market summary" page returned 404 when fetched directly, so the $6.41 figure rests on secondary confirmation. It is a widely published NATO figure.

## 2. Opening weekend, #1 finish, weeks in release

### Takeaway
The film opened **#1** with **$24,182,961** over the 3-day weekend, or $29.2M over the 4-day MLK holiday, on 2,524 screens. It was Samuel L. Jackson's biggest opening as a lead at the time, and the #2 basketball opening ever then, behind Space Jam. It ran **16 weeks** domestically, peaking at 2,574 theaters.

### Cited Findings
- 3-day opening of $24,182,961 (BOM) / $24,182,960 (The Numbers), ranked #1 (VERIFIED). [BOM](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0393162/); [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Coach-Carter-(2005))
- Opening details (REPORTED, BOM news article). [BOM article](https://boxofficemojo.com/article/ed2440430596/)
  - $29.2M over the 4-day MLK weekend.
  - 2,524 theaters and about 3,200 screens.
  - It beat Shaft ($21.7M) as Jackson's biggest lead opening.
  - Only Space Jam ($27.5M) had a bigger 3-day basketball-film opening.
  - It beat every coach or teacher true-story film listed, including Remember the Titans and Hoosiers.
- Audience and reception from Paramount exit polls (REPORTED): 52% female and 55% under 25; over 90% "definitely recommend"; 95% excellent or very good. [BOM article](https://boxofficemojo.com/article/ed2440430596/)
- Weekly domestic run (VERIFIED), from [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Coach-Carter-(2005)):
  - Weekly ranks for weeks 1–5: #1, #2, #6, #9, #12.
  - It stayed in theaters 16 weeks, from Jan 14 to the week of Apr 29, 2005.
  - Peak count was 2,574 theaters; 43 theaters remained in the final week.
  - Legs multiple 2.78; opening weekend was 36.0% of the total.
- Production budget was $45,000,000 (VERIFIED, The Numbers).
  - Worldwide gross was 1.7× budget.
  - Video release was June 21, 2005, through Paramount Home Video.

### Inferences
- About 48% of all domestic tickets were sold in week 1: $32.24M ÷ $67.26M = 47.9%, or about 5.03M tickets.

### Gaps
- Weekend-by-weekend data on the BOM release page could not be retrieved; the fetched URL pointed to a different title. The Numbers' weekly table was used instead.

## 5. Sports and basketball film rank (nominal and inflation-adjusted)

### Takeaway
- The "#3 inflation-adjusted" claim is **plausible only as "#3 among basketball films by tickets sold"**, behind Space Jam and White Men Can't Jump.
- It is **false as "#3 among all sports films"**: Rocky, The Blind Side, The Waterboy, The Longest Yard and others far exceed it.
- The nominal domestic rank among basketball films appears to be **#4**, behind Space Jam, White Men Can't Jump and Space Jam: A New Legacy.
- Neither ranking was verified on a primary chart this session; the sports and basketball chart pages returned 404.

### Cited Findings
- At opening, only Space Jam had a bigger 3-day basketball opening (REPORTED, BOM 2005). [BOM article](https://boxofficemojo.com/article/ed2440430596/)
- A RunRepeat guide ranks US basketball box office in this order: Space Jam, White Men Can't Jump, Coach Carter (REPORTED, no figures given, likely pre-2021). [RunRepeat](https://runrepeat.com/uk/basketball-movies-a-complete-guide)
- Space Jam (1996) is called the highest-grossing basketball film (REPORTED). [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/space-jam-tops-box-office-with-31-7m-opening/)
  - A search-summary snippet put AIR (2023) at "$89.2M domestic". That conflicts with widely reported figures (~$52M) and looks erroneous, so it is not used.
- Coach Carter's inflation-adjusted domestic gross is $125.5M, about 10.49M tickets (VERIFIED, The Numbers). [The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movie/Coach-Carter-(2005))

### Inferences
- From training knowledge, **NOT verified this session**: White Men Can't Jump about $76.3M (1992) and Space Jam: A New Legacy about $70.6M (2021). These would place Coach Carter **#4 nominal**.
- By ticket count, using the same NATO-price method:
  - Space Jam: about 20M+ tickets.
  - White Men Can't Jump: about 18M.
  - Coach Carter: 10.5M.
  - This puts Coach Carter at about **#3**, ahead of Space Jam: A New Legacy (about 7M), Hoosiers, Teen Wolf and Like Mike.
- **Re-verify** each rival's gross on BOM before publishing the claim.
