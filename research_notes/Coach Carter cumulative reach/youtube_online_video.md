- Clip networks are actively re-cutting the film. The Boxoffice "Sir/Madam" upload (Jan 2025) and the NOW PLAYING "Coach Claps Back" upload (Oct 2025) each passed 1.3–1.9M within months. The film is still generating new views in 2025–26.

### Gaps
- YouTube Shorts: the search scrape surfaced no Coach Carter Shorts with view counts. Shorts edits of the speech probably carry large view totals that this method could not see.
- YouTube search returns about 50 results per query, so the long tail of many small uploads (likely several million more combined) is not captured. Treat ~71M as a **floor**.
- The licensing status of the clip networks (Boxoffice, NOW PLAYING, Binge Society) was not verified.

## Q5. Watch-hour estimate

### Takeaway
ESTIMATE: Coach Carter YouTube content in the measured set (A–E, 71.3M views) has generated roughly **1.8M–3.3M watch hours (mid ~2.5M)**. Including the "Hope" soundtrack videos, the range is **~2.7M–5.0M (mid ~3.8M)**. These are floors, because the long tail, Shorts, TikTok and Instagram are excluded.

### Retention benchmarks used
- There is no official YouTube benchmark. A Retention Rabbit study of 10,000+ videos puts average retention at ~23.7%. Other guides call 45–55% average view percentage healthy for 8–12-minute videos. Median Shorts view-through is ~45%. Summarized in [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/), [quso.ai benchmarks](https://quso.ai/research/video-retention-benchmarks), [AIR Media-Tech definitions](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/watch-time-vs-average-view-duration) and [bytecap Shorts research](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks). These are REPORTED third-party figures, and the sources conflict.
- Short movie clips (2–5 min, a self-contained payoff scene) retain far better than long-form. Long podcasts and keynotes retain worse.

### Method
Hours = Σ(views × length in seconds) ÷ 3600 × retention, computed per video, then summed by category. Retention assumptions:
- Short clips and songs (A, B, C, C2, F): low 35% / mid 50% / high 65%.
- Long-form (D real Carter, E commentary/podcasts): 20% / 30% / 40%.
