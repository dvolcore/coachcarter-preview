## Wikipedia pageviews (film + Ken Carter, EN and other languages)

The English "Coach Carter" film article drew ~6.89M human pageviews from July 2015 through October 2026 (~600K/yr, peak 901K in 2024). The "Ken Carter" bio drew ~2.93M. - **ESTIMATE** — Film article views across all languages ≥ 6,885,551 (EN) + 3,755,452 (27 other editions) = **≥ 10.64M since mid-2015** (a floor; pl, zh and fa are missing). ## "Our deepest fear" scene (Marianne Williamson via Timo Cruz)

### Takeaway
The scene is the film's most circulated artifact. The site-linked YouTube clip alone logged ~7.85M views by Oct 2025. The speech also drives a long-running misattribution to Nelson Mandela, which keeps the film in quote-culture discussions.

### Cited Findings
- **REPORTED (prior playbook; third-party counter)** — The official-site-linked "Deepest Fear" YouTube clip had 7,847,424 views and 75,355 likes as of Oct 2, 2025. — playbook S04 (SocialCounts snapshot)
- **VERIFIED** — The passage is from Williamson's *A Return to Love*. Mandela never said it. The Nelson Mandela Foundation posted a correction in 2007, and Williamson's verified account objected in 2017 to CNN misattributing it to Mandela. — [Quote Investigator](https://quoteinvestigator.com/2019/06/30/deepest/)
- **REPORTED** — The film is widely credited with popularizing the passage. Townhall (2019, during Williamson's presidential run) ran a piece on Williamson being quoted in *Coach Carter*. — [Townhall](https://townhall.com/tipsheet/timothymeads/2019/06/28/this-2020-candidate-is-quoted-in-the-2005-film-coach-carter-do-you-know-which-one-n2549191)
- **REPORTED** — Shmoop indexes the quote under Mandela's "1994 Inaugural Address", which shows the misattribution persisting in educational quote sites. — [Shmoop](https://shmoop.com/quotes/deepest-fear-not-that-we-are-weak-powerful-beyond-measure.html)

### Inferences
- The 2019 Williamson campaign coverage probably boosted cross-traffic. Wikipedia peaks do not clearly show a mid-2019 jump, though, so this is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative measure of locker-room or curriculum use of the quote. Live YouTube counts for 2026 were not pulled.
