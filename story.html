The Story

He locked the gym.They unlocked their futures.

Richmond, California. January 1999. An undefeated team, a signed contract and a coach who believed the grades mattered more than the games.

Richmond Oilers · Season record at the lockout

Undefeatedwasn't enough.

Fifteen of his forty-five players had fallen short of the contract. Coach Carter padlocked the gym, cancelled practice and called off a game, and turned the team's gym time into study hall. A week later all forty-five met the standard — and the season resumed without a single forfeit.

The record, year by year
Timeline

From Richmond
to the world.

1959

Born February 13. Raised in Richmond.

One of nine children — seven sisters and a brother. He would go on to play for Richmond High himself, then build a business before coming home to coach.

1997

Takes over Richmond High

Head coach of the Oilers. Every player signs a contract, and so do their parents: a 2.3 GPA (above the 2.0 California required), every class, the front row, and at least ten hours of study a week.

1999

The lockout

Early January, the team is 13–0. Fifteen players are short of the contract. He padlocks the gym, cancels practice and calls off Friday's game. His own son, Damien, is among the players benched. A week later every player meets the standard and the season resumes — no forfeits. NPR, CNN, the New York Times and the Los Angeles Times cover it.

2001

Fifteen for fifteen

The Los Angeles Times reports that all fifteen players who missed the requirement have gone on to higher education.

2002

Olympic torchbearer

Carries the Olympic flame for Richmond and the Bay Area on the road to the Salt Lake City Winter Games — the moment he has called the greatest of his life. The same year he becomes head coach of the Los Angeles Rumble — SlamBall's official biography records his championship that season.

2005

Coach Carter opens at #1

Paramount Pictures and MTV Films release Coach Carter on January 14. It opens at number one with $24.2 million and reaches $76.7 million worldwide. Coach Ken Carter and Damien Carter serve as executive consultants. It opens the Palm Springs International Film Festival.

2012

Yes Ma'am, No Sir

February 28: Business Plus (Hachette Book Group) publishes his book, The 12 Essential Steps for Success in Life, with an audio edition read by Coach.

2015

The comeback

Around age 55, Coach goes back to college basketball — as a player. Baylor's official record for January 21, 2015 shows an 81–61 Baylor win over Huston-Tillotson: the game at the center of his comeback, and the true story behind Coach Carter Academy.

2023

Back to SlamBall

Head coach of the Rumble as SlamBall returns in Las Vegas.

2024

Home to Richmond

In December 2024, more than twenty-five years after the lockout, Coach is back with Richmond High. Local reporting in January 2026 described a handoff of the bench, with Coach preferring to help from afar.

2026

On display in Switzerland

In January, the FIBA Foundation features Coach Carter material in its basketball exhibition in Switzerland — the story, still traveling.

Today

The standard goes national

Universities, business summits, school districts, churches and youth organizations from coast to coast — and a new chapter on screen: Coach Carter Academy.

The original contract

The contract
that started it.

Nineteen points, by Coach's own count. Signed by every player. Co-signed by their parents — and by Coach. Enforced on the star, the bench and the coach's own son.

  • 2.3 GPAAbove the 2.0 California required.
  • Every classAttend all of them. Sit in the front row.
  • Game dayWhite shirt and tie.
  • The workHomework, study hall and community work.
  • 10 hoursOf study, every week.

Sources: Coach's book Yes Ma'am, No Sir; his 2005 interview; the San Francisco Chronicle and CNN, January 1999. The signed original hasn't yet been located in an authenticated scan, so we list only the terms on the record. About the original
